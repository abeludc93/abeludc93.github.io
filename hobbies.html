Hobbies & Interests

When I'm not doing data science or math, I spend my time exploring the skies (both virtual and physical) and digging into the digital archaeology of the 90s.

Aviation, MSFS & RC Planes

Flight simulation has been a passion of mine for years. From tweaking physics in Microsoft Flight Simulator to building and crashing Radio Controlled planes in the real world.

90s Computing & The Retro Web

I have a deep appreciation for the early days of personal computing and the Web 1.0/2.0 era. I spend time spinning up old Windows machines on VirtualBox, exploring the CARI Institute aesthetics, and browsing archived websites to see how things used to be built.

My Retro Bookmarks:

  • The Old Net - A portal to browse the early web exactly as it was in the 90s and 00s.
  • Protoweb - A free public service that hosts historical internet sites.
  • List of Coronet Films - Wikipedia archive of classic educational films.
