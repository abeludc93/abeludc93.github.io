Interesting Bookmarks

A curated list of sites, videos, and resources that I find educational and worth sharing.

Programming
Resource Description
yet another insignificant Programming Notes A personal favourite containing programming notes and tutorials covering various languages (mostly Java) and concepts such as OOP, Databases, Webapps. Beginner friendly explanations from the ground-up. A must from Chua Hock-Chuan from the NTU University.
Material Curricular Libre Open educational resources and materials for web and software development (in Spanish). It also hosts a number of computer science magazines. Author: Bartolomé Sintes Marco.
Calmcode.io Short, clear, and calm video tutorials focusing on individual tools and libraries within the Python ecosystem. Comfy stuff. From Vincent D. Warmerdam, who's also a founding enginner of the much-beloved Marimo notebooks and whose personal blog (koaning.io) I also recommend.
MDN Web Docs T·H·E reference platform for consulting and learning standard web development.
CPSC 223: Data Structures and Programming Techniques This is a massive 2022 book from Yale. It covers all there is to know on both C programming and foundational data structures. I did not read the whole lot but it is quite useful for consulting thanks to its detailed table of contents - indexing around 800 pages.
Mathematics
Resource Description
HyperMath Concept maps and summary explanations of mathematical principles, applied statistics and their links to problems in physics and astronomy. Old but gold - © 1998 C.R. Nave from Georgia State University.
No Bullshit Textbooks A collection of books written by Ivan Savov, meant for students & self-learners. It styles a straight-to-the-point attitude on calculus, mechanics, and linear algebra. Upcoming releases include statistics, physics and an engineering guide. I've only read the linear algebra look so far, but I loved it. It's a huge book that covers many concepts: you really can't get more for that price. I recommend checking the first pages of his books online and downloading his printable tutorials for free.
Braver New Math Seth Braver - mathematics professor in the South Puget Sound Community College - has published a series of books with fresh perspectives on learning mathematics that I'm particulary fond of. I began with his 'The Dark Art of Linear Algebra: an intuitive geometric approach', but I have since read 'Full Frontal Calculus' and 'Precalculus Made Difficult', all of which are really exceptional. A simple, humoristic and unique style that speaks highly of its author. Currently reading his 'Lobachesky Illuminated' to shed some light on non-Euclidean geometry once and for all.
Penn Online Learning Calculus A good YouTube series on Single Variable Calculus from the University of Pennsylvania's by Prof. Robert Ghrist.
3Blue1Brown I've got nothing else to say: the best animated visual explanations of a multitude of mathematical concepts. Grant Sanderson single-handledly developed manim, a graphics library for creating mathematical animations. His famous YouTube channel is golden.
MatematIA Blog of Universidad de Sevilla professor Fernando Sancho Caparrini, with detailed entries on a suite of computer science topics, mathematics and modelling; a real treat. There are also full courses on logic, machine learning, multi-agent modelling and much more.
Cálculo en una variable (UDC) Collection of Jupyter notebooks covering single-variable calculus topics from Universidade da Coruña (in Spanish).
Cálculo en varias variables (UDC) Collection of Jupyter notebooks covering multivariable calculus topics from Universidade da Coruña (in Spanish).
Algebra I (UCLM) Course materials for Algebra I by Enrique Castillo at UCLM (available in English & Spanish).
Algebra II (UCLM) Course materials for Algebra II by Enrique Castillo at UCLM (available in English & Spanish).
Physics
Resource Description
HyperPhysics A broad and comprehensive exploration of different physics topics trough concept maps. © 2024 C.R. Nave from Georgia State University.
Javalab A rich collectin of interactive science simulations and virtual experiments for educational purposes. Technology and mathematics demos can also be found. As far as I know, this is an old website created in 1996 by South Korean teacher Dong-joon Lee. As the name suggest, the first simulations were actually Java applets, but they have been completely remade w/ HTML5.
Electric Circuits A free series of textbooks on the subjects of electricity and electronics. © 2000-2023, Tony R. Kuphaldt.
Circuit Simulator A useful, free and completely online electrical and electronic simulator written in JavaScript. (Paul Falstad / Iain Sharp)
Machine Learning
Resource Description
Cursos IA - Fernando Sancho Caparrini Teaching materials and courses on Artificial Intelligence, Logic, and Agent Modelling with Julia, NetLogo & Prolog (in Spanish). From University of Seville professor Fernando Sancho Caparrini, whose blog I also emphatically recommend.
Notas de Apredizaje Automático Profundo Notes of Machine Learning by Mariano Rivera, in Spanish (CIMAT © 2022, Mexican Mathematical Research Center).
Applied Machine Learning in Python Excellent guide to machine learning by Andreas Müller, core contributor to scikit-learn, based on the course on ML that he held at Columbia, which I also recommend. It is a bottom-up free book which makes heavy use of notebooks and practices.
Applied Machine Learning (Columbia) You can find here the slides and video lectures for Andreas Müller's AML course at Columbia University. I recommend reading the slides or watching the corresponding video lectures before diving in the respective chapter from the previous link.
Machine Learning for Intelligent Systems Course site for CS4780 at Cornell University by professor Kilian Weinberger. This course may seem old nowadays (Fall 2018), but it contains great materials and video lectures on different machine learning topics.
Machine Learning and Pattern Recognition This is the homepage for Yann LeCun ML course at NYU (Fall 2010). I picked the last edition of said course, but there are available older versions from 2004. I've yet to review these, but it would be an interesting idea for a future post. Of course, practices involve LeCun's programming language, LUSH, a dialect of Lisp. I'll be covering these language, and some of the demos available, as well as a guide for its installation on modern machines in a future entry.
CS229: Machine Learning Stanford's legendary machine learning course taught by Andrew Ng. I've linked the current edition (Summer 2026). Search for the older Summer 2022 for Andrew's opera prima.
Introduction to Machine Learning Detailed notes on ML basics, including SVMs, from slama.dev.
Deep Learning
Resource Description
Neural networks and Deep learning A highly recommended free online book by Michael Nielsen explaining the core principles of neural networks.
ML and DL courses Collection of didactics and teaching materials by Alfredo Canziani.
Deep Learning in Scientific Computing Course from ETH Zurich focusing on the interdetails of deep learning and scientific simulations (2023).
Deep Learning Book The definitive textbook on deep learning by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville (MIT Press).
CS230: Deep Learning Stanford's deep learning course taught by Andrew Ng.
Sebastian Raschka's YouTube Channel Excellent video lectures and tutorials on deep learning and machine learning implementation.
The Little Book of Deep Learning A compact, phone-formatted introduction to deep learning by François Fleuret.
Practical Deep Learning for Coders Fast.ai's renowned top-down approach to learning and applying deep learning quickly.
Understanding Deep Learning Online book providing a comprehensive and modern overview of deep learning concepts.
